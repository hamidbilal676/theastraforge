Online training videos for courses and teams

Whether you're selling a course, onboarding employees, or training customers to succeed with your product, the lesson format is the same problem: expertise trapped in someone's head, or in a 40-slide deck nobody finishes.

We turn your material — slides, documents, raw recordings, or just an outline — into structured video lessons: a presenter or voiceover carrying the teaching, motion graphics for the concepts, captions throughout, and chapter cards that break the course into finishable pieces. Full course builds run $30 per finished minute, lesson by lesson, so a course can launch with module one while module five is still in production.

Creators building a channel around teaching also pair training content with our talking-head editing for face-to-camera teaching.

Tutorial videos that end support tickets

Every 'how do I…' email your support team answers for the hundredth time is a tutorial video you haven't made yet. A good tutorial is ruthlessly clear: the goal stated up front, the screen zoomed to the exact button, each step narrated as it happens, and the common mistake flagged before the viewer makes it.

Send us access to your product (or your own screen recordings) and the questions your users keep asking. We script the walkthrough, record or clean the screen capture, add callouts and voiceover, and deliver a tutorial your support team can simply link to. Software teams usually start with the five most-asked questions — those five videos quietly delete a meaningful share of tickets.

Tutorials live naturally next to explainer videos: the explainer sells the product in 90 seconds; the tutorial teaches it in five minutes.

Website promo videos that turn visits into signups

Your homepage gets seconds. A promo video is how you spend them: the problem, your solution, the proof, and the next step — compressed into 60-90 seconds above the fold, in your brand's voice and visuals.

We make website promo videos in the format that fits your brand: an AI avatar presenter guiding the story ($50 per finished minute), an animated promo with motion graphics (quoted per project), or a short-form style promo cut from your existing assets (from $15 per short). Every promo ships with captions — homepage videos autoplay muted — and in the ratios your site and socials need.

Send us your homepage URL. We'll reply with promo angles for your business and a free sample segment before you commit to anything.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me to start?

Whatever you have: slides, a document, raw recordings, product access for tutorials, or just your website URL for a promo. We handle scripting and production from there.

Who does the voiceover?

Professional AI voiceover is included, matched to your brand's tone and paced for learning. Prefer your own voice? Send the recording and we'll build the video around it.

How much do training and tutorial videos cost?

$30 per finished minute, including voiceover, graphics, captions and chapters. Website promos start at $15 per short-form cut; avatar-led promos are $50 per finished minute; fully animated promos are quoted per project.

Can you build a whole course?

Yes — lesson by lesson, with consistent branding, intros and chapter cards across the course. You can launch with the first module while the rest are in production.

What if my product changes after the video is made?

We re-record or re-render just the affected steps and scenes. Your training and tutorial library stays current without reshooting everything.

How fast is turnaround?

within 5 hours of your requirements as standard, with one revision round included. Larger course builds run on a rolling schedule we agree up front.
