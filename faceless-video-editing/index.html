Who faceless editing fits

Brands with a product to explain, creators who would rather not film, coaches and educators turning knowledge into content, and anyone sitting on footage that never became a video. If your message works without your face, faceless editing is usually faster and cheaper than filming — and the videos are easier to scale, because one script can become a long video plus a week of shorts.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to film anything?

No. Faceless videos are built from script, voiceover and visuals. If you have footage, we will use it; if you don't, we build the visuals from stock, motion graphics and AI visuals.

What is the difference from your faceless YouTube videos service?

This is editing only — you send material, we return finished videos. The faceless YouTube videos service adds niche research and channel-format production, and YouTube automation adds full channel management on top.

How much does it cost?

Shorts are $15 each. Long-form faceless videos are priced per finished minute at our standard editing rates, and monthly packages start at $199/mo. Start with a free sample edit.

Who owns the videos?

You do — 100%, including the right to publish them anywhere. We work as your editing team.
