What AI avatar videos are (and aren't)

An AI avatar video features a photorealistic AI presenter speaking your script directly to camera, with accurate lip-sync, natural head movement, and expressive delivery. It looks like a filmed spokesperson segment — because the underlying performance is modeled on real human presentation.

What it isn't: a cartoon, a robot voice reading text, or a gimmick. Modern avatar quality has crossed the line where viewers engage with the message instead of questioning the medium — which is why agencies now use avatars for ads, explainers, and training at serious volume.

The honest limitation: avatars don't improvise. They deliver your script faithfully, which makes them perfect for scripted content and wrong for anything that needs genuine spontaneity. For unscripted authority content, our talking-head video editing is the better fit.
