About The Astra Forge

Who we are

The Astra Forge is an AI video editing agency for US brands, founded in February 2026. We help US businesses turn raw footage and scripts into scroll-stopping video — talking-head edits, AI UGC ads, AI avatars, short-form video, and product videos.

Our workflow is AI-assisted: fast turnaround (48-hour turnaround per video), consistent quality, and one revision round on every video. We have delivered 500+ videos for creators and brands.

How we work

  1. Send footage — Raw clips, a script, or just an idea. Phone footage is fine.
  2. We edit with AI — AI-assisted editing: cuts, captions, color, voiceover, motion graphics.
  3. You review — One revision round included. We polish until it converts.
  4. Scale — Retainer pipeline: fresh videos every week, on autopilot.
