Why video podcasts need a different edit

A proper video podcast edit is several jobs stacked together. Multicam sync and switching. Filler removal — on video, every dead second is visible. Audio cleanup, because video podcast audio is usually worse than audio-podcast audio (room echo, uneven speaker levels, background hum). And packaging for YouTube: an intro hook, lower thirds, end screens, chapters and show notes.

Bad multicam editing is worse than a single locked-off camera, and un-packaged episodes die on YouTube no matter how good the conversation was. The edit is what turns a recording into a show — timestamped chapters that become searchable segments, descriptions that rank, and an opening that signals "this is worth your time" in the first 15 seconds.

Already have episodes published as audio only? The same stack applies to your best back-catalog episodes when you re-release them on video.
