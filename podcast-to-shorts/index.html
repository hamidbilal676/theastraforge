A workflow built for weekly shows

Consistency is the whole game in podcast growth, and consistency dies in editing backlogs. Our repurposing workflow is designed for shows that publish weekly: episode goes live, we pull 3-5 clips per episode, shorts go out across the week driving traffic back to the full episode.

The math is friendly. At $15 per short, four clips per weekly episode costs $240/month — less than one sponsored post, for content that compounds. The Growth package at $499/mo covers 30 shorts monthly, which handles a weekly show with room for extra cuts from guest highlights or solo segments.

Frequently asked questions

My podcast is audio-only. Can you still make shorts?

Yes. We create audiogram-style clips with animated waveforms, captions, and your cover art. Strong moments carry the format — video is a bonus, not a requirement.

How many clips do you get from one episode?

Typically 3-5 strong clips per hour-long episode, depending on content density. We'll tell you honestly how many your episode supports — we'd rather deliver 3 great clips than 6 weak ones.

How fast is turnaround?

within 5 hours of your requirements as standard. Weekly shows run on a rolling schedule: episode drops, clips come back, shorts publish all week.

Do you need my video files or just links?

Links are fine — RSS feed, YouTube, Spotify, Drive, Dropbox. Whatever's easiest; we'll pull what we need.

What does it cost?

$15 per short, or bundle into monthly packages: Growth at $499/mo covers 30 shorts, plenty for a weekly show. First sample clip is free.
