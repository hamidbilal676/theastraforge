Short-Form Video Editing for Reels, TikTok & YouTube Shorts

Short-form is where attention lives now — and where most brands post content that gets scrolled past. The difference between a short that dies at 200 views and one that travels isn't luck. It's the edit: the hook in the first second, the pacing that never lets attention drop, the captions that hold muted viewers.

The Astra Forge edits short-form video for US brands at $15 per short: Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts cut hook-first, captioned, and finished within 5 hours of your requirements. Send raw clips, a long video to cut down, or just an idea — we handle the rest.

One revision round. No commitment. Free sample edit to prove it.

The anatomy of a scroll-stopping short

Every short we cut is built around the same skeleton, because the platforms reward the same behavior: a hook in the first 1-3 seconds that earns the next 10, pacing with a cut or visual change every few seconds, and captions styled to be readable at a glance.

We open with your strongest moment — not your logo, not an intro, the payoff or the provocative claim — then structure the remaining seconds to deliver on the hook's promise. B-roll, zooms, sound effects, and on-screen text keep the eye moving. Music is chosen for energy, not just background fill.

This isn't guesswork; it's the editing pattern behind the shorts that consistently outperform. Whether the source is your talking-head footage, a podcast episode, or raw phone clips, the edit follows what the algorithm rewards: retention. Pair this with our podcast-to-shorts service if your long-form content should be feeding your short-form channels.

Repurpose what you have, or start from scratch

Most of our short-form work starts from footage you already own: a webinar, a talking-head video, a podcast episode, even a screen recording. We find the moments worth clipping and cut them into standalone shorts that make sense without the original context — each one captioned, paced, and formatted for 9:16.

Starting from scratch works too. Send raw phone clips — a product demo, a behind-the-scenes moment, a quick tip — and we'll shape them into finished shorts with hooks, captions, and music. Fitness coaches send workout clips (see our fitness industry page); ecommerce brands send product footage that becomes AI UGC-style ads.

Either way, you approve a free sample edit first. If the style isn't right, we adjust before you've spent anything.

Volume without the quality drop

Posting three times a week beats posting once a month — but only if every post holds the standard. That's the failure mode of cheap editing: the first videos look great, then quality slides as volume rises. Our workflow is built to prevent exactly that.

Every short goes through the same pipeline: hook check, caption accuracy pass, audio leveling, format verification for each platform. Batch your clips and they move through on a rolling 5-hour turnaround, so a month of content can be produced in days, not weeks.
