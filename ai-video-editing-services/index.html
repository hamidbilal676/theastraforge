Why brands switch to AI video editing services

Speed. 48 hours per video is our standard turnaround; 24-hour rush is available at +25%. Traditional post-production quotes in weeks. When a trend has a 72-hour window, speed is the whole game.

Volume economics. A single US freelance editor typically charges $2,000–$5,000/month for 10–20 videos with 5–10 day turnarounds. Our monthly packages deliver 15–50 shorts plus talking-head and avatar minutes for $199–$999/mo — 2–3x the volume at a fraction of the cost per video.

Consistency. One team, one style guide, one quality bar across every video. No re-briefing a new freelancer every month, no style drift, no disappearing editors.

No overhead. No salaries, no benefits, no software licenses, no gear. You pay per video or per month, and you can pause or cancel anytime.

The honest caveat: AI-assisted editing is not magic. It excels at speed, volume, and consistency — the 90% of content work that is production. Genuinely creative hero pieces still benefit from human-first direction, which is why every video goes through our human review pass before delivery.
