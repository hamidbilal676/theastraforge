## The creative testing math

Paid social is a volume game now. The ad that won last quarter is already fatiguing; the algorithm rewards accounts that feed it new concepts constantly. Brands scaling past six figures a month in ad spend typically test dozens of creative variations monthly: new hooks on winning angles, new angles on winning offers, new formats for new placements.

Traditional UGC can't sustain that cadence. Every new creative means finding a creator, briefing them, waiting, revising, and paying — then paying again when usage rights expire. The per-creative cost stays high no matter how many you order, because each one is a relationship, not a production run.

AI UGC changes the unit economics. Once your product assets are in our system, a new creative angle is a production task: new script, new AI performer, new hook — same 48-hour turnaround, same flat price. Your testing velocity goes from "as fast as creators reply" to "as fast as you can think of angles."

## How our AI UGC ads are made

**The script comes first.** You send a finished script, a rough outline, or just the offer and the audience — we shape it into a direct-response structure built around a 3-second hook, because UGC ads are won or lost in the opening frame. The hook makes a claim or asks a question the viewer can't ignore; the body proves it fast; the CTA is explicit.

**Then the performer.** We match an AI creator to your brand — tone, age range, energy, setting — and they deliver your script with natural lip-sync and pacing. Not robotic, not uncanny: a performance calibrated to feel like the creator content your audience already watches.

**Then the edit.** This is where ads become ads: dynamic word-by-word captions, product b-roll cutaways timed to the script's claims, pattern interrupts every few seconds, trending-audio-aware music beds, and a clean CTA end card. Every ad passes our review checklist — hook strength, caption accuracy, audio levels, format checks for Meta, TikTok, and Shorts placements — before you see it.

**Then you scale.** One winning angle becomes ten variations: new hooks, new openers, new CTAs, new formats. Creative testing finally moves at the speed of your ideas.

## Ad formats we produce

One script, many placements. Every concept we produce ships in the formats your media buyer actually needs:

- **9:16 vertical** — the default for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Hook-first, captioned, safe-zone framed.
- **1:1 square** — for Meta feed placements, where square still earns its keep on thumb-stopping power.
- **16:9 landscape** — for YouTube in-stream and any placement where horizontal wins.

We also produce format variations as part of testing: the same angle with three different hooks, the same hook with two different openers, UGC-style versus polished cuts of the same script. Variation is the raw material of creative testing, and producing it is where the AI pipeline earns its keep — what takes a creator roster a week takes us an afternoon.

Honest answer: AI UGC is not a replacement for everything. If you need a hero brand film with genuine human emotion and a real story, hire real creators and a real crew. That's not this service.

What AI UGC is for is the other 90% of creative testing: hook variations, angle tests, offer tests, seasonal refreshes, and the constant churn of concepts your media buyer needs to find winners.
