## What Drives the Price?

### 1. Video length and complexity

A 15-second captioned Reel costs a fraction of a 3-minute product demo with 3D animation. Most AI editing services tier by output volume, not runtime — but complex motion graphics and 3D still command premiums.

### 2. Turnaround speed

Turnaround is the other lever: most services charge rush fees for anything fast. We keep it simple — 5-hour delivery is our standard, with no rush fees.

### 3. Creative input required

"Edit this footage" is cheapest. "Concept, script, and produce from scratch" costs more — you're buying creative direction, not just editing. AI UGC ads (script → AI actors → finished ad) sit in the middle: no filming, but real creative work.

### 4. Volume

This is where AI economics shine. The per-video cost collapses with volume:

- 1 video: $15–$50 each (pay per video)
- 15 videos/month: ~$13 each (Starter retainer, $199/mo)
- 30+ videos/month: ~$12–$17 each (Growth retainer, $499/mo)

Traditional editing can't match this curve — human hours don't scale. AI does.

### 5. Revisions

1–2 revision rounds per video is standard and included. Unlimited revisions usually signals no process (and slower delivery).

## How to Avoid Overpaying

1. **Start with a free sample edit.** Judge quality on your footage before spending anything.
2. **Match the tier to your cadence.** Don't buy 30 videos/month if you post 8. Start small, scale up.
3. **Go monthly, not annual.** Retainers should be pausable. Annual lock-ins are obsolete.
4. **Count cost per published video**, not per month. A $499 Growth plan delivering 30 shorts = ~$17 per short. That's the number that matters.
5. **Beware "AI-powered" markups with no portfolio.** If they can't show work, the AI is probably just an auto-edit app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

If you publish 8+ videos monthly: absolutely. That's ~$13 per finished short with 5-hour turnaround — versus $150+/video and week-long waits from freelancers.

AI compresses the mechanical work (transcription, rough cuts, captions, B-roll sourcing) from hours to minutes. You pay for creative direction and output, not labor hours.

Reputable global agencies charge flat USD rates regardless of client location — the same $199 plan serves the USA, UK, Australia, and Europe.
