Animation that explains without filming

Some products can't be filmed easily — they're too small, too technical, or the magic happens inside. That's where animation earns its keep: exploded views, labeled diagrams in motion, before-and-after sequences, and feature highlights that would need a macro studio to capture on camera.

We blend animation with your real footage so the video feels grounded, not cartoonish: your actual product on screen, with animated layers showing what the eye can't see. It's the format behind our sample above — a skincare device explained from inside the skin.

If your product needs a person presenting it instead, our AI avatar videos put a spokesperson on screen, and our AI UGC video ads turn the same assets into ad creative.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to ship my product to you?

No. We build from your existing assets — product photos, clips, your product page. In almost every case, that's everything we need.

30-90 seconds for product page heroes; under 30 seconds for ad cuts. We'll recommend the right length for your placement when you send the product.
