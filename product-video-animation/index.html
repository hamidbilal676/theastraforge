Product Videos & Animation That Answer What Photos Can't

Product photos show what something looks like. Video shows what it does, how big it really is, how it moves, and why it's worth the price. That's the gap video fills on a product page — and it's the gap costing you sales if it's empty.

The Astra Forge makes product videos and animations for ecommerce brands: demo videos, feature callouts, unboxing-style cuts, and UGC-style ads, all built from your existing photos and footage. No product shipment, no studio shoot. Finished in 48 hours per video.

From a single hero video for your bestseller to a full catalog refresh, we turn your assets into video that sells.

The product videos we make

Demo videos walk through your product in action — how it works, what it feels like, the details photos flatten. Feature-callout videos isolate the three or four things that justify the price, with motion graphics labeling each one as it appears on screen. Animation that explains without filming

Some products can't be filmed easily — they're too small, too technical, or the magic happens inside. That's where animation earns its keep: exploded views, labeled diagrams in motion, before-and-after sequences, and feature highlights that would need a macro studio to capture on camera.

We blend animation with your real footage so the video feels tangible, not cartoony. A serum bottle gets a slow rotating beauty shot; then animation labels the ingredients as each benefit appears. A kitchen gadget gets a real demo clip; then motion graphics break down the mechanism.

The result is the best of both: the trust of real product footage, the clarity of animation. Tell us what your product does that's hard to photograph, and we'll show you the video treatment on a free sample.

From your assets to finished video

Send a link to whatever you have — product photos, existing clips, your product page, even competitor videos you admire (we'll make yours better, not copy theirs). Tell us which product, which angle, and where the video will live: product page, ads, Amazon listing, or social.

We script the structure, cut your assets into a hook-first flow, add motion graphics, captions, licensed music, and color polish. You review within 48 hours per video with one revision round, and the finished file arrives in the aspect ratios you need.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to ship my product to you?

No. We build from your existing assets — product photos, clips, your product page. In almost every case, that's everything we need.

What video length works for product pages?
