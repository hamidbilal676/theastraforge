Frequently asked questions

Do I ever need to appear on camera?

Never. Faceless videos are built entirely from script, voiceover and visuals. There is no filming at all — that's the point of the model.

Which niches work best faceless?

History, finance, true crime, motivation, tech, top-10 lists and explainers — any niche where the value is the information, not the host's personality. We'll validate your niche before we start.

Will faceless videos get monetized?

Faceless channels are fully allowed on YouTube. What gets rejected is reused, low-value mass content — every video we make is original, script-first and edited to monetization-policy standards. No honest service can guarantee monetization or income, and we won't pretend otherwise.

Who owns the videos and the channel?

You do — 100%, including the channel and all ad revenue. We work as your production team.

How much does it cost?

Shorts are $15 each; long-form faceless videos are priced per finished minute at our standard editing rates, and monthly production plans start at $199/mo. Start with a free sample video for your niche.

Can you run the whole channel for me?

Yes — production plus uploading, metadata, Shorts and monthly analytics is our YouTube automation service. This page covers production; management is one upgrade away.
