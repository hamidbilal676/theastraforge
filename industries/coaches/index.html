Video Marketing for Coaches: Authority Content Without the Editing Grind

Coaching clients buy transformation, and transformation is sold on trust. Trust comes from watching you teach — consistently, across their feed, week after week. The coaches growing fastest aren't more qualified; they're more visible.

The Astra Forge is the editing team behind coaches' content engines: talking-head edits, short-form clips, podcast repurposing, and course content. You record; we handle everything else. From $15 per short, 5 hours per video.

Your content engine, handled

The winning coach playbook is simple: long-form authority content (talking-head videos, podcast episodes) feeding short-form distribution (Reels, TikToks, Shorts) feeding your funnel. Simple — and a full-time editing job if you do it yourself.

We run the whole pipeline. Talking-head video editing at $30 per finished minute turns your recordings into polished authority videos. Faceless video editing turns any idea into faceless videos. Short-form editing at $15 per short keeps your feeds active daily.

Batch a week's recordings in one afternoon; finished content flows back on a rolling 5-hour-per-video turnaround. You stay in your zone of genius — coaching — while your content compounds.

Shorts that fill your funnel

One strong teaching moment, cut right, outperforms a month of motivational quotes. We find the quotable insights, contrarian takes, and mini-teachings in your long-form content and cut them into hook-first shorts with captions that hold muted viewers.

Every short ends with your call to action — follow, DM, book a call — because views without a next step are vanity. The Growth package at $499/mo (30 shorts + 5 minutes talking-head) is the sweet spot for coaches posting daily; Brand at $999/mo scales to full content-team output.

Consistency beats virality. A year of daily shorts from your real expertise builds an audience no ad budget can buy.
