Video Marketing for Dental Practices That Fill Chairs

Patients choose dentists they trust — and trust is built on faces, not stock photos. A 60-second video answering "does it hurt?" does more for your booking calendar than any ad copy.

The Astra Forge edits dental video marketing for US practices: patient education videos, practice tours, doctor introductions, and FAQ content that turns anxious searchers into booked appointments. You record on a phone; we deliver polished, captioned video in 5 hours.

We go deeper on strategy in our guide to dental video marketing with AI.

Answer the questions patients are already asking

"Does a root canal hurt?" "How much is Invisalign?" "What happens at a first visit?" — your front desk answers these daily, but your website doesn't. FAQ videos put your doctor's face on the answers, and a calm, clear 60-second explanation converts anxious browsers into callers.

We edit your recordings into clean, captioned FAQ videos with on-screen text reinforcing key points. Our AI avatar videos can also front educational content at $50 per finished minute — useful when the doctor's schedule can't fit filming.

These videos work everywhere: your website's FAQ page, Google Business Profile, Instagram, and YouTube. One afternoon of recording becomes months of trust-building content.

Show the practice, not just the services

New patients are nervous about the unknown: what the office looks like, who'll greet them, whether it feels modern and clean. A 90-second practice tour video answers all of it before they ever call.

Send us walkthrough clips and team introductions; we cut them into a warm, professional tour with music, captions, and smooth pacing. Doctor intro videos get the talking-head editing treatment — filler cut, captions added, authority intact.

This is the content that makes a patient choose you over the practice with the slightly better reviews. Familiarity converts.

Frequently asked questions

Our doctor is camera-shy. Can you help?

Yes — we edit out the awkward pauses and filler, and AI avatar videos can deliver educational scripts with no filming at all. Many clients start avatar-led and move to on-camera once comfortable.

Can you handle HIPAA-sensitive content?

We only edit what you send us. Keep patient footage out of your recordings (or get written consent first), and stick to doctor-led educational content — that's what performs best anyway.

What video length works for dental content?

30-60 seconds for FAQs and social; 60-90 seconds for practice tours and doctor intros. We'll recommend the right cut for each placement.

How fast is turnaround?

5 hours per video as standard. Batch your recordings and build a content library in weeks.
