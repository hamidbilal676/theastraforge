Video Marketing for Real Estate Agents Who Want the Listing — and the Next One

Buyers scroll listings. They stop for video. A listing with a walkthrough video, an agent they recognize from Reels, a market update that actually explains something — that's the agent who gets the call when it's time to sell.

The Astra Forge edits real estate video for US agents and teams: listing walkthroughs, talking-head market updates, neighborhood guides, and agent brand content. You shoot on your phone; we make it look like a production. 5 hours per video, one revision round, free sample edit.

Listing videos that make buyers book the showing

A photo gallery shows rooms. A walkthrough video sells the feeling of walking through the front door. We cut your raw walkthrough footage into paced, captioned tours with smooth transitions, music, and text overlays for price, beds, baths, and the details buyers filter for.

Agent on camera? Even better — a 15-second intro from you before the tour builds the personal connection that wins listings. Our talking-head video editing polishes those segments at $30 per finished minute, and short-form editing cuts 30-second teaser versions for Reels and TikTok at $15 per short.

In a market where every agent posts the same photos, video is the differentiator — and consistent video is the brand.

Become the local expert on camera

The agents dominating their farm area aren't just selling houses — they're publishing. Monthly market updates, neighborhood tours, "what $500K buys here" breakdowns: this is the content that makes you the obvious choice when someone decides to move.

We make the publishing part effortless. Record a 5-minute market update on your phone; we return a polished video with captions, b-roll of the neighborhoods you mention, motion graphics for the stats, and short-form cuts for every platform. Batch a month's content in one afternoon of recording.

One revision round keeps your voice intact, and the 5-hour-per-video turnaround means your market update is still relevant when it publishes.
