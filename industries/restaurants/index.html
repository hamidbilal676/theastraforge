Video Marketing for Restaurants: Make Them Taste It Before They Book

Nobody picks a restaurant from a text menu anymore. They pick the place whose pasta pull stopped their scroll, whose chef they recognize from Reels, whose Friday special they saw that morning.

The Astra Forge edits restaurant video marketing: mouth-watering food shorts, dish showcases, chef features, event promos, and review-style content. You shoot on a phone in the kitchen; we make it look delicious. $15 per short, 5 hours per video.

The tasty details: restaurant video marketing with AI.

Food that moves sells tables

A still photo of your signature dish is nice. A 15-second video — steam rising, cheese pulling, sauce pouring — is a reservation. We cut your kitchen footage into crave-inducing shorts with pacing, close-ups, sound design, and captions that name the dish and the offer.

Chef features and behind-the-scenes content build the personality that turns first-timers into regulars. Our short-form editing handles the full rhythm: daily specials, new menu drops, event promos, all at $15 per short.

For paid reach, AI UGC video ads put creator-style energy behind your promos — "this spot just opened and you need to try it" creative that fills seats on slow nights.

A week's content from one shoot afternoon

You don't need a videographer in the kitchen weekly. One afternoon of phone footage — five dishes plated, the chef talking through two specials, the dining room at golden hour — becomes two weeks of content in our hands.

We cut hero dish videos, teaser shorts, chef talking-head segments (polished here), and story-format cuts, all captioned and on-brand. Batch it monthly and your feed never goes quiet, even during your busiest service weeks.

Menu change? New seasonal dishes get the same treatment within 5 hours per video. Your content stays as fresh as the menu.
