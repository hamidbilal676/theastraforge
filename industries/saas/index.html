The format guide: SaaS explainer videos with AI editing.

Explainers that end the confusion

Your homepage has eight seconds to answer "what does this do and why should I care." An explainer video answers it in 90: the painful status quo, your product as the turning point, the transformed outcome — visualized with motion graphics and real UI.

Our explainer videos service scripts, storyboards, and produces animated, avatar-led, or hybrid explainers. AI avatar-led versions run $50 per finished minute; full custom animation is quoted per project. Every project starts with a free 15-20 second sample of your actual script.

Place the finished explainer on your hero, pricing page, onboarding sequence, and ads. One asset, working every channel, for years.

Demo content and feature launches, on demand

Every feature launch deserves video: a 60-second walkthrough beats a changelog nobody reads. Send screen recordings (or just the feature and access notes); we cut them into polished demos with captions, callouts, and pacing that respects the viewer's time.

AI avatar videos front these walkthroughs when a human guide beats pure screen capture — same presenter across your whole library, updated by re-render when the UI changes. No re-shoots when v2.1 ships.
