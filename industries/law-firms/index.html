Frequently asked questions

Is video appropriate for serious practice areas?

Yes — when edited with restraint. We keep legal content professional: clean cuts, accurate captions, no gimmicks. Educational videos build trust; hype destroys it, so we never do hype.

What about client confidentiality?

We edit only what you send. Keep client details out of recordings, stick to general educational content, and have your standard disclaimers ready — we'll include them as on-screen text.

Can you help if our attorneys are too busy to film?

Yes. AI avatar videos deliver your scripts professionally with zero filming time. Many firms use avatars for FAQ volume and reserve on-camera for attorney intros.

How long should legal videos be?

60-90 seconds for FAQs and social; 2-3 minutes for attorney intros and detailed explainers. We'll advise per topic when you send your list.
