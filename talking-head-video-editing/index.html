Talking-Head Video Editing That Makes You Look Like the Authority You Are

You know your stuff. Your audience can tell — when they actually watch. The problem is the gap between recording a video and publishing one worth watching: the ums, the dead air, the flat delivery, the hour of editing for every ten minutes of footage.

The Astra Forge edits talking-head videos for coaches, consultants, and creators across the US. You record — on your phone, your webcam, Zoom, whatever you've got — and we turn it into polished, captioned, retention-engineered content in 48 hours per video. One revision round, finished-minute pricing, no hourly billing surprises.

Your expertise is the product. Our edit is the packaging.

Your expertise deserves better than raw uploads

Viewers decide in seconds whether to keep watching, and raw talking-head footage rarely survives that decision. Long pauses, filler words, and static framing signal "amateur" before your first insight lands — which is unfair, because the insight is the whole point.

Professional talking-head editing removes everything between your viewer and your message. We cut filler words and dead air, tighten the pacing, add subtle zooms and cuts that keep the eye engaged, and layer in b-roll or on-screen text exactly when a point needs emphasis. The result feels like you, on your best day, every time.

This is the format that builds authority businesses: course creators, business coaches, consultants, and educators whose content has to look as credible as their advice. If your videos also need to travel as shorts, our podcast-to-shorts and short-form video editing services cut them down for every feed.

What's included in every edit

Every talking-head edit starts with a full cleanup pass: filler words, false starts, long pauses, and tangents removed, with cuts placed to keep your natural rhythm. Then the polish layer — color correction, audio leveling and cleanup, and noise reduction so a bedroom recording sounds like a studio.

Engagement comes next: styled captions throughout (because most viewers watch muted at first), pattern-interrupt zooms, b-roll or screen recordings cut in where they reinforce your point, motion graphics for key frameworks or steps, and a clean branded intro/outro if you want one.

You review the finished video within 48 hours, request your revision round, and publish. Pricing is $30 per finished minute — a 10-minute video costs $300, whether your raw footage was 12 minutes or 40. See all pricing or get a free sample edit of your own footage.

One polished video is nice. A publishing rhythm is what grows an audience. That's why our talking-head workflow is built for batch: send a week's worth of recordings at once, get them back on a rolling 48-hour-per-video turnaround, and keep your content calendar full without touching an editor.

Coaches posting daily or a few times a week pair this with our monthly packages — Growth at $499/mo includes 5 minutes of talking-head plus 30 shorts, and Brand at $999/mo scales to 10 minutes of talking-head, 50 shorts, and 5 minutes of AI avatar. Send footage from anything — we handle the rest

Phone, webcam, DSLR, Zoom recording, Riverside, StreamYard — if it records video, we can edit it. You don't need a studio, a microphone upgrade, or perfect lighting. Some of the best-performing talking-head content on the internet was shot on a phone in a living room; what separates it is the edit.

Send a link to your footage (Drive, Dropbox, or our upload link), tell us your style preferences or send a reference video you like, and we'll match it. First order includes a free sample edit so you can see exactly what your footage looks like in our hands before you commit to anything.
