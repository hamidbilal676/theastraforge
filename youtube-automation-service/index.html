We manage your channel, not just your videos

Production is only half of YouTube automation. The other half is showing up every week — publishing on schedule, packaging every upload properly, reading the analytics and adjusting. That is the half owners quit on, and it is the half we take over completely.

You keep full ownership. The channel, the AdSense account and 100% of the revenue stay in your name. We work inside your channel as your production and management team — you never hand over your business, just the busywork.

Already have a channel that stalled? We will audit it free and tell you honestly whether it is worth reviving — or whether a fresh niche would pay back faster. And if you only need production while you manage the channel yourself, short-form editing is available on its own.

Why channels stall — and how a managed pipeline fixes it

Inconsistent uploads. The algorithm rewards channels that publish on a rhythm. A managed pipeline never misses a slot because the owner got busy.

Weak first 30 seconds. Most faceless videos lose half their viewers before the one-minute mark. Our scripts and edits are built backwards from the retention graph.

No feedback loop. Uploading without reading analytics is guessing. Monthly reviews turn every video into data for the next one — topics, hooks and thumbnails improve on evidence, not hope.

One-person burnout. Research, scripting, voiceover, editing, thumbnails, uploading, metadata — six jobs. Owners who try to do all six usually quit by month three. Our clients do one job: approve the videos.

Frequently asked questions

What does a YouTube automation service actually do?

We run the production and operations of a faceless YouTube channel for you: niche research, scripts, AI voiceover, editing, thumbnails, uploading, metadata, Shorts repurposing and analytics reviews. You own the channel and keep 100% of the revenue.

Do you manage existing YouTube automation accounts, or only new channels?

Both. We manage YouTube automation accounts for clients who already have channels — including stalled ones — and we set up new channels from scratch. Management covers uploads, titles/descriptions/tags, scheduling, thumbnails, Shorts and monthly analytics.

Is YouTube automation allowed by YouTube?

Yes — faceless channels are fully allowed. What YouTube prohibits is reused or mass-produced low-value content. Every video we produce is original: original script, original edit, real value for the viewer, made to monetization-policy standards.

Who owns the channel and the ad revenue?

You do — 100%. The channel and AdSense stay in your name; we work as your production and management team. We never take a cut of your ad revenue.

How many videos can you produce per month?

Most managed channels run 8–12 long-form videos a month plus Shorts from each one. Volume depends on your package — monthly plans start at $199/mo, and we will agree a calendar you can sustain.

How do we start?

Tell us your niche — or let us recommend one from our research — and we will produce a free sample video so you can judge the quality before committing to anything.
